# 🏔 Simon Hilton's Mountains Framework

> *"When you look back on your life, will you see mountains or plains?"*

---

## The Idea

Plains are fine. But they're forgettable. You can be perfectly comfortable on plains — good income, pleasant days, nothing wrong. But when you turn around at 80, it all blurs together. The texture is gone.

Mountains are different. They're distinguishable because they *cost something*. The effort is what makes them memorable. Which means the goal isn't to make life easier — it's to make it **worth remembering.**

Life seems to be about choosing some mountains. So when you look back, you have a sense of triumph.

Each quarter you camp. And from camp, you look back. Did you see mountains or plains?

Not every quarter needs a new mountain. Some take years. The camps are just stopping points on the way up. Plains have a purpose too — recovery happens on flat ground, and the valleys between climbs matter.

The enemy isn't failure. It's **drift** — ending up on the plains without choosing them.

---

## The System

Four layers:

```
☁️ Clouds      — someday, on the horizon. Hold lightly, review annually.
🏔 Mountains   — the permanent why. The things worth climbing.
⛺ Camps       — where you are this quarter, on the way up.
👣 Steps       — what you do today. Lives in your task manager of choice.
```

Three rituals:

```
🌅 Morning Check-in  — open Claude, say "morning". See your steps for the week.
📅 Weekly Review     — every Saturday. Did my steps lead up, or did I walk on plains?
📆 Annual Review     — every January. Which clouds became mountains? Which dissolved?
```

## The Five Mountains (example)

These are the five areas most people find their real mountains live in. Your specific mountains will be different — the reflection skill helps you find yours.

| Mountain | The Why |
|---|---|
| 🏠 Be present with my family | Memories with the kids, intimacy with my partner, showing up for extended family as a model not just a provider |
| 💰 Build lasting financial freedom | Lock in the target with comfort and margin, never be trapped by a paycheque |
| ✍️ Create things that matter | Books, courses, ideas — things that exist in the world after you |
| 🤝 Stay connected to friends | Invest in the people who matter, don't let busyness be the excuse |
| 🌍 Live a full life | Read widely, stay curious, see live music, hike mountains, look at stars |

## The Four Skills

Install all four skills in Claude. They work together as a system.

### 1. `mountains-reflection`
**Start here.** A guided conversation that helps you find your actual mountains — the things you'd genuinely regret not doing. Also surfaces your clouds — the someday things you want to hold but aren't ready to commit to. Takes 30-60 minutes the first time.

**When Claude uses it:** *"Help me figure out what matters to me"*, *"I want to build a life OS"*, *"what should I actually be working toward"*.

### 2. `mountains-quarterly-setup`
**Run each quarter.** Maps your camps to mountains, scans your calendar for events that belong in the plan, flags which mountains have no footsteps, and builds your full Notion + TickTick structure — including Mountains database, Camps database, Clouds page, Personal CRM, and the ⛺ Camps TickTick project with mountain sections.

**When Claude uses it:** *"Help me plan my quarter"*, *"set up my camps"*, *"map my goals to my mountains"*.

**Requires:** Notion. Google Calendar. TickTick (or any task manager).

### 3. `mountains-weekly-review`
**Run every Saturday.** Pulls completed steps from your task manager, maps them to mountains, checks your Personal CRM for overdue connections, gives an honest read on which mountains got steps and which didn't, pre-loads next week's mountain steps into ⛺ Camps, and surfaces one deliberate action for each neglected mountain.

**When Claude uses it:** *"Weekly review"*, *"review my week"*, *"how did my week go"*.

**Requires:** TickTick (or task manager with completed task history). Notion. Personal CRM.

### 4. `mountains-booking`
**Run whenever you have an unbooked camp.** Turns intentions into calendar events, task reminders, and restaurant bookings. Because an unbooked camp is just a wish.

**When Claude uses it:** *"Book that in"*, *"add it to the calendar"*, *"find somewhere to eat near X"*, *"make this happen"*.

**Requires:** Google Calendar. TickTick. Notion.

## Getting Started

**Step 1 — Install the skills**

Download all four `.skill` files and install them in Claude via Settings → Skills → Upload Skill.

**Step 2 — Run the reflection**

Open Claude and say: *"I want to use the Mountains Framework to figure out my mountains."*

Claude will guide you through a 30-60 minute conversation. Come with your current goals list if you have one.

**Step 3 — Set up Notion**

Claude builds your Mountains hub page in Notion — the Mountains database, Camps database, Clouds page, and Personal CRM.

**Step 4 — Set up TickTick**

Claude creates a ⛺ Camps project in TickTick with five sections — one per mountain. This is where your steps live.

**Step 5 — Plan your quarter**

Share your current goals list and Claude will map everything to mountains as camps, identify gaps, assign dates, and pre-load steps into ⛺ Camps.

**Step 6 — Book the mountain moments**

For every camp that isn't booked, Claude will make it real — calendar events, reminders, restaurant suggestions, whatever it takes.

**Step 7 — Set your weekly review**

Pick a time (Saturday afternoon works well) and set a recurring reminder. Every week: open Claude, say "weekly review", and get an honest read on your mountains.

**Step 8 — Start your morning check-in**

Each morning, open Claude and say "morning". Claude will pull your open steps from ⛺ Camps, show what's due this week, and suggest where to focus your session.

## The ⛺ Camps TickTick Project

Create a project called **⛺ Camps** in TickTick with five sections:

```
⛺ Camps
├── 🏠 Family
├── 🤝 Connection
├── ✍️ Creation
├── 💰 Financial
└── 🌍 Life
```

Every step you take toward a mountain lives here. This is what you open at 5:30am. This is what the weekly review checks.

---

## The Personal CRM

The Connection mountain dies without a system. Create a Personal CRM in Notion with:

- **Name** — the person
- **Cadence** — Weekly / Fortnightly / Monthly / Quarterly
- **Last Contacted** — date of last contact
- **Next Contact** — formula: Last Contacted + Cadence

During the weekly review, Claude checks who's overdue and surfaces them as connection steps in ⛺ Camps for the coming week. When you reach out, update Last Contacted. Simple.

---

## The Regret Minimisation Test

Before committing to anything significant, ask: *At 80, would I regret not doing this?*

This is the filter. Not every activity needs to pass it — maintenance is fine. But your mountains should all pass it clearly.

---

## A Note on Plains

Plains aren't failure. They're just forgettable. Some weeks are plains weeks — recovery, maintenance, keeping the lights on. That's fine and necessary.

The goal isn't to turn every moment into a mountain climb. It's to make sure you're *choosing* the plains, not drifting there.
